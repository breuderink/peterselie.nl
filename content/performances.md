All shows mentioned below can be adapted for people with special needs.

**[Mouse wants House](/muis)**<br>
Mouse has found an apple and wants to take it into his little hole, but the apple is too big. So he goes out to look for a bigger house.<br>
Interactive narrative show with table puppets. And there is plenty of singing in it as well.<br>
age-group: 2 years and up

**[All the ducks...](/alleeendjes)**<br>
On the bank of the ditch in my garden there lives a duck. And I  am going to let you into a secret: she has built  a nest of twigs and feathers. And then she went and sat on it and laid an egg.<br>
In the show we watch the duckling being hatched and follow it on its way into the wide world.<br>
During the show matching children's songs will be sung with guitar accompaniment.<br>
age-group: 2 years and up

**[Once more](/nogeenkeertje)**<br>
The join-in show "Once more" is a true party for a maximum of 30 pre-schoolers!<br>
First the story is played in a small setting with puppets: the little bear is going on a picnic and later on when it is cold and dark outside put to bed by his mother.<br>
Afterwards each child can have a picnic with a (loan) teddybear of his own and with wind and rain outside we all together crawl in the bear cave and put the little bears to bed.<br>
The emphasis is on empathy and stimulation of the senses. There proves to be a strong appeal to the caring qualities of the children.<br>
We will sing quite a lot of songs.<br>
age-group: 2 years and up

**[On the star and the stable](/kerstvoorstelling)**<br>
With reference to well-known Dutch carols, the Christmas story is told in simple language and illustrated by large standing puppets.
